ABOUT THIS SITE

Research-focused AI news without a paid backend

This website is hosted entirely through GitHub. GitHub Actions periodically retrieves public RSS metadata from selected research and technology sources, classifies the stories, removes duplicates, and generates the news feed.

The site links readers to the original publishers. It does not reproduce complete external articles.

01

03

Every card directs readers to the original source.

04

Zero lab infrastructure

No lab server, GPU, backend database, or always-running computer is required.
